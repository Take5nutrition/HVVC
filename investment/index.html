What's Included

Season Fees

Season Fees Cover

  • Tournament registration fees
  • Power League Series participation
  • Friendship tournaments
  • Regional competition fees
  • Monthly coaches stipend
  • Practice facility fees

Uniform Package

  • 2 game jerseys
  • 2 practice shirts
  • 1 Nike Pro Spandex
  • 1 pair Nike knee pads
  • 1 HVVC sweatshirt
  • 1 team ball bag
Purchased separately from tuition. The 10-1 jersey is included.

Payment Structure

  • Deposit due at acceptance
  • 5 monthly payments, due on the 1st (Dec–Apr)
  • LeagueApps (card or bank), cash, or check
  • Late fee: $25 after 5 days
Travel/lodging for overnight tournaments is optional and communicated in advance.
How to Pay

Payment Details

01

Deposit

Your deposit is due at acceptance after tryouts to secure your athlete's spot.

02

Monthly Payments

The remaining balance is split into five equal payments, due on the 1st of each month, December through April.

03

Payment Methods

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, or bank payment through LeagueApps, with autopay set up at tryouts. Cash and checks are also accepted.

04

Questions?

Have a payment question? Contact us and we'll help.

Financial Aid

Scholarship Opportunities

We believe finances should never be a barrier to playing the sport you love.

HVVC Scholarship Fund

  • Based on financial need
  • Application opens October 15
  • Deadline: November 30 at 9 PM
  • Notifications by December 17
  • Distributed in January
  • Spread across remaining payments
Cannot be used for tryout fees, CEVA memberships, or initial club deposits.

CEVA Athlete Success Fund (CASF)

  • External scholarship through CEVA
  • Columbia Empire Volleyball Association
  • Applications open October 1
  • Open to all eligible athletes

Contribute to Scholarships
