Season Tuition Includes
  • Team practices and gym rentals
  • Head and assistant coaching, as applicable
  • Entry fees for tournaments on your team’s published schedule
  • Practice shirts and one TIY hairband
  • Team equipment and training supplies
  • Hudl Core: 12-1, 13-2, and 14-2
  • Hudl Plus: 13-1, 14-1, 16-1, 16-2, and 18-1

10U is a developmental program with one weekly practice, no tournaments, and one included game jersey.

Uniform Package, Purchased Separately

Families on teams 11U through 18U purchase their required uniform package directly through our team store. Packages cost approximately $305–$320 per player, depending on sizing.

  • One long sleeve game jersey
  • Two short sleeve game jerseys
  • Nike hooded sweatshirt
  • Nike jogger sweatpants
  • Nike Pro spandex
  • Nike kneepads
  • Nike backpack

Practice shirts are included in club tuition.

10U receives one club-provided short-sleeve pink jersey. Additional apparel is optional.

Payment Schedule
  • Team-specific deposit due when accepting your offer
  • Five monthly payments, December through April, due on the first of each month
  • Monthly payments processed automatically through LeagueApps, with autopay set up at tryouts
  • Payment in full accepted at tryouts. No pay-in-full discount is offered
  • Cash, check, or any payment method accepted through LeagueApps
  • No additional payment processing fees
  • Tuition assistance available by application

Additional Expenses

The following costs are separate from season tuition:

  • Required uniform package for teams 11U through 18U, approximately $305–$320 per player, depending on sizing. Purchased separately through our team store. 10U’s game jersey is included in tuition.
  • Athlete travel and lodging
  • Coach travel costs, assessed separately by team
  • Additional tournaments, including Nationals if your team earns and accepts a bid, involve extra entry fees and related travel costs. Dates and locations vary by age group. Team entry fees will be communicated once confirmed and divided among participating players.

Travel details and estimated costs will be communicated in advance.

Tuition assistance is available by application.
