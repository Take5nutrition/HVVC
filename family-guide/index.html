Dear HVVC Families,

Welcome to Happy Valley Volleyball Club! We offer competitive club volleyball and HVVC Academy programs, creating opportunities for athletes of all skill levels to develop, compete, and grow.

Founded by Sarah Borski in October 2023 with one team and 12 athletes, HVVC is preparing to grow to 10 teams and 120 players this season. Our commitment remains the same: quality coaching, positive culture, meaningful competition, and affordability.

Experienced Coaches. Meaningful Growth.

Our coaches see potential in every athlete and are committed to helping it grow. They pair high expectations with clear instruction, individual feedback, and encouragement. Through purposeful training and game situations, athletes learn how to execute skills, when to use them, and how to make confident decisions on the court.

We’re looking for athletes who put the team first, bring contagious energy, embrace coaching, and celebrate their teammates’ success as loudly as their own. Players ready to work hard, take ownership, and persevere through challenges.

Work • Ownership • Will

Effort matters. Accountability is expected. Perseverance is celebrated.

Our Leadership & Culture

High Expectations. Real Support.

Sarah Borski · Founder & Club Director

Coaching athletes since 2009

Sarah brings state championship, national club, and NCAA Division I experience to HVVC. At Gresham High School, she helped lead consecutive Oregon 4A championship teams, including an undefeated season and a No. 12 national ranking.

A national club runner up and CEVA regional champion, she earned a full athletic scholarship to Boise State University, competing as a Division I outside hitter in 2003 and 2004.

Sarah has coached club, school, and recreational volleyball since 2009. As a director, coach, and volleyball parent, she helps players build confidence, become strong teammates, and love the game. Her “Warm Demander” philosophy balances high expectations with encouragement, care, and accountability.

Our Mission

To develop confident, competitive athletes through intentional coaching, positive culture, and a commitment to growth on and off the court.

Our Vision

To become the most intentional, athlete-centered volleyball club in the Pacific Northwest, developing players who compete with confidence, lead with character, and love the game for life.

Our Core Values

  • Positive Culture: Supporting athletes as they learn and grow.
  • Elite Development: Providing purposeful practice, quality instruction, and meaningful feedback.
  • Team First: Emphasizing communication, shared goals, and accountability.
  • Competitive Excellence: Competing with purpose, learning from challenges, and improving together.

Payment Schedule & Options

Deposit due at acceptance. Five monthly payments, December through April, are due on the first. LeagueApps autopay is set up at tryouts. Families may pay in full at tryouts; no discount is offered. Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and bank payments are accepted through LeagueApps. Cash and checks are also accepted.

Plan For Additional Costs

Uniform packages are approximately $305–$320 for 11U–18U. Athlete travel, lodging, and team coach travel are separate. Added tournaments, including Nationals, may require additional fees. 10U’s pink game jersey is included in tuition.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, HVVC believes finances should not prevent athletes from accessing meaningful volleyball opportunities. Tuition assistance is available by application.

Before You Arrive

  1. Register with HVVC. Complete LeagueApps registration, pay the listed tryout fee, and finish all registration waivers. Save your confirmation. Register for tryouts.
  2. Confirm the correct age group. Use the 2026–27 USAV age chart, which uses birth date rather than grade alone. Contact Sarah with placement questions.
  3. Activate your CEVA/USA Volleyball membership. Purchase a current 2026–27 membership through SportsEngine before participating. The girls’ tryout membership costs $20 plus processing fees, covers tryouts through December 31, and can be credited toward a full-season upgrade. HVVC registration and CEVA membership are separate. Get your membership.
  4. Prepare forms and health information. Complete the USAV Player Medical Release and all waivers requested in registration, including membership, liability, conduct, SafeSport/MAAPP, and concussion/SCA acknowledgements where prompted. Keep a signed medical release copy ready and follow HVVC’s submission instructions. Download the medical release.

Pack Your Bag & Check In

Bring proof of membership, registration confirmation, completed forms, court shoes, knee pads, athletic clothing, a water bottle, and any personal medical supplies. Arrive at your age group’s check-in time ready to learn, compete, and support others.

If offered a roster spot: Review the CEVA Letter of Commitment and HVVC agreement before signing. Your team deposit is due at acceptance (see Season Dues). Upgrade a tryout-only membership before practices or tournaments. Athletes who are 18 or turn 18 before August 31, 2027, should follow CEVA’s SafeSport-required membership instructions.
