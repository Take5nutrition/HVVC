HVVC Scholarship Fund

Happy Valley Volleyball Club is committed to helping athletes access the opportunities, growth, and team experience that club volleyball provides. The HVVC Scholarship Fund offers financial assistance toward club dues for families experiencing financial hardship.

Funding is limited. While we strive to support as many families as possible, submitting an application does not guarantee an award.

November 18, 2026, at 9:00 PM Pacific Time

Incomplete applications and late submissions will not be considered.

Scholarship Details

Scholarship Amounts

Scholarship amounts may vary significantly based on each family’s financial circumstances, the number of eligible applicants, and available funding. Requests are reviewed individually, and neither funding nor a specific award amount is guaranteed.

Awards apply exclusively toward club dues. Scholarships do not cover initial club deposits, tryout fees, or CEVA memberships.

Eligibility & Deposit Requirements

Families may submit an application before tryouts. Applications will be reviewed after the November 8 and November 15 tryouts, once team rosters have been finalized. Only applications from athletes selected for an HVVC roster will be considered.

All athletes, including scholarship applicants, must pay the initial club deposit at tryouts upon accepting a roster spot. Applying for assistance does not waive or defer this payment.

Recipients must complete all required commitment documents and contracts and remain in good standing under club policies and the Financial Commitment Contract.

Scholarships are awarded for the current season only. Families must reapply each year.

Financial Need & Confidentiality

We recognize that financial hardship looks different for every family. Applications consider household responsibilities, changes in income, significant expenses, and the assistance needed for an athlete to participate.

Families do not need to receive public assistance to be considered. The application includes an optional financial need checklist and a required written explanation of the family’s circumstances. An athlete essay, video, or recommendation letter is not required.

Please do not submit tax returns, pay stubs, bank statements, or other sensitive financial documents.

Application information will be treated confidentially and reviewed only by those responsible for administering the scholarship program.

How Awards Are Applied

Scholarship funds are credited directly to the athlete’s HVVC account and divided evenly across the five monthly installments from December through April. Funds are not paid directly to families.

Families remain responsible for fulfilling their Financial Commitment Contract and paying any remaining balance. Payments must continue as scheduled unless HVVC provides an updated payment schedule in writing.

How to Apply

A parent or guardian must complete the online application in full, including household information, an explanation of financial need, the amount requested, and information about other assistance requested or received.

Additional Assistance

Families may also explore the CEVA Athlete Success Fund, a separate program with its own application, eligibility requirements, and deadlines. Applying for an HVVC scholarship does not automatically apply your athlete for CEVA assistance.

Please report other assistance requested or received on your HVVC application and notify us if its status changes.

Questions? Please contact hvvc@hvvcvolleyballclub.com.
